# Demonstration application

Read root AGENTS.md. Use Jetpack Compose and consume the SDK as a Gradle library.
Design: a premium scientific instrument, obsidian/charcoal, warm copper/sand and
restrained cyan. Clear readable controls and accessibility take priority.

Display only real SDK capabilities, capture progress and measured evidence.
Unloaded models and uncalibrated decisions must be clearly unavailable. Graphs
may only render captured/derived data. Guide four registered positions A-D.
Never claim enrollment/verification success before SDK operations complete.
Motion may reflect real SDK busy/stage state only. Result marks must follow
completed reference persistence or the actual available decision; scans without
validated comparison remain `INCONCLUSIVE`. Do not show match/not-found states
or animations before calibrated decision behavior exists.
Keep raw capture and physical labeling explicit. No confidence percentages until
calibrated, no mock objects or scripted success. Test lifecycle and microphone
permissions on the actual device. Screenshots must come from the real application.

User-directed showcase layout: keep main screens sparse; no visible V6 label or
model-training explanation on them. Put hardware/model readiness, true operation
logs, scientific details and capture configuration in a hamburger-only sidebar.
Do not offer unvalidated MATCH/CHANGED decisions; Scan is research capture and
must remain `INCONCLUSIVE` without trained models/calibration, clearly reported
in the sidebar.

The judge-facing showcase accepts any stable box/object through the
`showcase_object` two-side flow: name (enrollment only), real side-1 capture,
turn prompt, real side-2 capture, approve/retry/reject, then encrypted
uncalibrated research reference plus provenance digest. Only completed
references appear as enrolled; partial/rejected runs do not. Never present the
digest as calibrated identity recognition.
